# FootIntel Methodology & Validation Notes

## 1. Data Source & Coverage Audit
- **Database:** `data/processed/footintel.duckdb` built from `dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets`.
- **Tables:** `transfers` (175,165 rows), `games` (88,958 rows), `players` (50,149 rows), `player_valuations` (656,301 rows), `clubs` (796 rows), `competitions` (65 rows), `appearances` (1,894,350 rows), `game_events` (1,274,469 rows), `game_lineups` (3,179,016 rows).
- **Season Alignment:** `transfers.transfer_season` (`'22/23'`) maps to `games.season` (`2022`). Analysis window spans `2012–2025` (`2012-08-10` to `2026-05-24` for Top 5 leagues; `2012-09-18` to `2026-05-06` for UCL, where the 2025/26 Final is not yet in `games`).
- **Historical Transfer Coverage Bias (Survivorship / Scrape-Recency):** 100.0% of rows in `transfers` belong to players with `players.last_season >= 2023` (the Transfermarkt API transfer scraper only covers players active in 2023+). Cross-referencing `appearances` in Top 5 leagues (`2,476–2,721` active players/season) shows active-player transfer coverage rises from **16.7% in 2012/13** and **67.0% in 2019/20** to **87.6%–100.0% in 2022/23–2025/26**. Historical spending curves prior to 2022/23 therefore represent **tracked-cohort spending** rather than complete market totals.

## 2. Analytical Design & Safeguards
1. **Season-Aligned League Membership:** Domestic league spending trends (Analysis 1) and standings (Analysis 2) derive club league membership per season from `games`, avoiding misleading attribution from static `clubs.domestic_competition_id`.
2. **Normalized Sporting Metrics:** Because Bundesliga plays 34 matches, Ligue 1 played 28 matches in 2019/20 and switched to 34 matches in 2023/24, and England/Spain/Italy play 38 matches, regressions use Points Per Game (`ppg`) and within-season normalized position percentile (`pos_pct`) alongside raw `points`.
3. **Zero-Spend & Log Scaling:** Positive-spend log regressions use `ln(gross_spend_m)` (`gross_spend > 0`) to avoid the `log1p(0)` artificial leverage gap; full-sample models use `asinh(gross_spend_m)`, linear `gross_spend_m`, and Spearman rank correlation.
4. **Estimated Market Value Gain (Non-Financial):** Analysis 5 bounds `player_valuations.date` strictly to `[transfer_date, next_transfer_date)` and groups by canonical `clubs.club_id` / `clubs.name`. Metrics are named `est_peak_mv_gain`, `peak_mv_to_fee_multiple`, `est_latest_mv_gain`, and `latest_mv_to_fee_multiple` to distinguish paper valuation appreciation from realized financial profit/ROI.
5. **Non-Causal Econometric Framework (Pooled vs Club Fixed Effects):** Pooled OLS (`R² = 0.2076`) and Club-Averaged Cross-Sectional OLS (`R² = 0.3861`) capture strong **between-club associations** (wealthier clubs both spend more and earn more PPG). Club Fixed Effects (`within R² = 0.0002`, `p = 0.745`) and Two-Way Club+Season FE (`within R² = 0.0047`, `p = 0.055`) show that **single-season transfer fluctuations within the same club** have virtually no immediate linear association with PPG. Standard errors are club-clustered (Liang-Zeger sandwich estimator). Results describe predictive associations, not causal treatment effects.
6. **Reproducibility & Verification:** Run `python -m analysis.run_all` and `python -m unittest tests.test_phase2_analysis -v` from `backend/` (8 independent verification tests recalculating statistics directly against `footintel.duckdb`).
